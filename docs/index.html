Install

  1. Click Download Murmur above, then open Murmur.dmg from your Downloads folder.
  2. Drag Murmur onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
  3. Open Applications and double-click Murmur. macOS says it “could not verify” the app. Click Done.

    That's expected. Murmur is a small private app, not from the App Store.

  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the line about Murmur and click Open Anyway. Confirm with Touch ID or your password, then click Open.
  5. A waveform icon appears in the menu bar at the top right. Murmur downloads its speech model (190 MB, a minute or two) and shows “Murmur is ready”.
  6. When macOS asks about Accessibility, click Open System Settings and switch Murmur on. This lets Murmur paste for you. The first time you dictate, also Allow the microphone.
Every day

Dictate

Click where the text should go, an email or a chat message, then press ⌥ Option Space and start talking. The panel shows what Murmur hears while you speak. When you're done:

⏎ ReturnPaste the text where your cursor is.
⇧ Shift ⏎Paste with the accurate model. A second or two slower, better with names and punctuation. Download it once from the menu bar icon (550 MB).
⎋ EscCancel. Nothing is pasted.
Panel buttonsPaste, Copy (copy only, paste it yourself later) or Cancel.

Murmur detects the language on its own. If ⌥ Space clashes with another app, pick a different shortcut from the menu bar icon → Shortcut.

What it can and can't see

Privacy

Your voice stays on your Mac

Speech is turned into text by a model running on your Mac. Murmur only goes online to download that model.

It doesn't watch your typing

Murmur reacts only to its own shortcut, and to Return, Shift-Return and Esc while you're dictating. It never asks for Input Monitoring.

Good to know

Start it with your Mac

System Settings → General → Login Items → + → choose Murmur.

Remove it

Quit from the menu bar icon, delete Murmur from Applications, then delete ~/Library/Application Support/Murmur.
