Murmur

Talk instead of typing. Murmur turns your speech into text in any app, entirely on your Mac. Nothing you say is sent anywhere.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

1. Install

  1. Drag Murmur onto the Applications folder in this window.
  2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Murmur.
  3. macOS will say it “could not verify” Murmur. That's expected: Murmur is a small private app, not from the App Store. Click Done.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Murmur and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open.

You only need to do this once.

2. First start

  1. A waveform icon appears in the menu bar at the top right. That's Murmur. It has no window.
  2. Murmur downloads its speech model (about 190 MB). The icon shows the progress. Wait for “Murmur is ready”.
  3. macOS asks to allow Murmur to control your computer (Accessibility). Click Open System Settings and switch Murmur on. This lets Murmur paste the text for you. If you skip it, Murmur copies the text and you press ⌘ V yourself.
  4. The first time you dictate, macOS asks for the Microphone. Click Allow.

3. Dictate

  1. Click where you want the text, e.g. an email or a chat message.
  2. Press ⌥ Option + Space and start talking. A small panel at the top right shows what Murmur hears, live.
  3. When you're done:
⏎ ReturnPaste the text where your cursor is.
⇧ Shift + ⏎Paste using the accurate model: a little slower, better with names and punctuation. Download it once from the menu bar icon (about 550 MB).
⎋ EscCancel. Nothing is pasted.
ButtonsThe panel also has Paste, Copy (just copy it, don't paste) and Cancel.

Murmur understands many languages and detects the language by itself.

Tips

  • If ⌥ Option + Space is used by something else, pick another shortcut from the menu bar icon → Shortcut.
  • To start Murmur automatically: System Settings → General → Login Items → + → choose Murmur.
  • To remove Murmur: quit it from the menu bar icon, delete it from Applications, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Murmur (the speech models).

Privacy

Your voice is turned into text on your Mac. Murmur only connects to the internet to download its speech models (from Hugging Face). It doesn't read what you type: it only reacts to its own shortcut, and only while you're dictating to ⏎, ⇧⏎ and Esc.
